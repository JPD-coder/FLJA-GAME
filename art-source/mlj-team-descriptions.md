# Major League Jai Alai team descriptions

From the MLJ investor deck, slides 19-23.

## Peloteros

Dive into the heart of Miami's electrifying energy with the Peloteros, the essence of jai alai - a sport where the pelota, the fastest ball in play, whips through the air, igniting a spectacle of speed, exhilaration, and a touch of peril. Every match is an adrenaline-fueled dance that keeps spectators riveted, holding their breath as the pelota propels them to the very edge of excitement. Immerse yourself in the rich tapestry of South Florida’s jai alai heritage that don the era's hallmark Baby Blue and Hot Pink, a vibrant homage that captures the soul of both the game and Miami Vice lifestyle. For those who savor the clever twist of phrases, our name's playful nod to both the athletes and the spirited ball itself is sure to “tickle your pelotas”. We are the jai alai aficionados, the originals who've cherished the sport from its inception a century ago when it captured our hearts like the streets of Miami.

## Squared Groupers

Get ready to dive into the thrilling, untamed world of jai alai, where the spirit and illusiveness of South Florida's legendary square grouper lives on! These mysterious packages that once bobbed in the coastal waters, symbols of the region’s infamous wealth, wild nights and even wilder stories, are a testament to an era where the scenes and parties were legendary. South Florida jai lai, with its fast-paced action and high-stakes thrills, captures the essence of those glory days. It's a sport that's seen its fair share of colorful characters, the kind who've become icons of mystery and mavericks of the streets. With our jai alai Club, you don’t even need to like the sport, lol – your love for the “cowboy” days of Miami and lust to relive the lifestyle of one the most infamous eras of our time represents our lifestyle brand. With us, every match is more than a game—it's a nod to the past, a toast to the daring, and a celebration of all that makes South Florida the place of legends.

## Skunk Apes

Embark on a wild journey with the Skunk Apes, the elusive guardians of Florida's untamed wilderness. As these mythical beasts are to sightings, so are the true Florida Natives to the land—rare, resilient, and revered. They share an unbreakable bond with the state’s scenic treasures and the authentic Florida way of life. Just like the spirited game of jai alai, the Skunk Ape's legend is resurging, embraced by a team that's as tenacious and tough as they come. If you're a “swamp thang” who thrives in the great outdoors, or if you take pride in the “good ol' boy” way of life, then our team is your call to the wild. For those who prefer the grit over the gloss, the rugged over the refined, our team is your sanctuary. Join us as we stand tall against the "pretty boys" of the league—our sights are set on victory in the fast-paced, exhilarating world of jai alai. With the Skunk Apes, it's not just about the game; it's about heritage, it's about camaraderie, it's about claiming the legacy that's rightfully ours.

## Don Juan Marquis

Embrace the allure of the legendary Don Juan, the quintessential symbol of Spanish charm and romantic conquest. Our team, Don Juan “The Marquis,” is not just a name; it's a celebration of passion, art, and the enigmatic spirit of seduction that characterized the original Don Juan’s escapades. We draw inspiration from jai alai's rich origins in the Basque region, infusing the narratives of love, bravado, and allure that resonate with the very essence of Spain. Join us as we embody the spirit of Don Juan's tales - of ardent lovers and the quintessential Latin charm that stirs the soul. Whether you see yourself as a modern-day “Latin lover" or a connoisseur of the game seeking the thrill of connection, the Don Juan "The Marquis" are calling. For our fans in pursuit of passion, let every game be your stage to express yourself and chase the connection between the sport and your favorite players true love that awaits. But beware, The Don Juan’s are the pretty boys of the league out to score so if you find yourselves heartbroken – Don’t hate the player, hate the Game!

## Seamen

Just like the masterful captains and crews who navigate the vast Atlantic, our team is synonymous with skill, leadership, and a deep connection to the coastal waves that crash a stone's throw from where our jai alai heroes battle it out. For those just discovering the dynamic world of jai alai, the game may seem as enigmatic and alluring as the legends of the Bermuda Triangle and the fabled city of Atlantis, which lie hidden beneath the waters near our very shores. If the thrill of the unknown calls to you, let the mighty Atlas usher you through the intriguing journey of jai alai. Embrace the camaraderie, the excitement, and the mystery that comes with being part of a club that's as much about discovery as it is about victory. With the Seamen, every match is a voyage into the heart of a sport as deep and captivating as the ocean itself.

## Invaders

Step into the domain of the Invaders, the audacious renegades of the jai alai world. In the depths of Florida's blue waters, they emerged as the formidable adversaries, the "bad boys" of the sea, disrupting the ecosystem just like they're set to disrupt the league. With a flair for the dramatic and a rep for being the tough guys on the court, the Invaders are the one team everybody loves to hate but can't ignore. Marking the centennial of jai alai's Florida debut, the Invaders squad storms into the scene carrying the spirit of a sport that's endured four centuries, landing on Florida shores with a splash that still ripples through time. Love them or loathe them, like the indomitable spirit of jai alai and the unstoppable surge of the villainous lionfish, this team is here to stay. Rooted in the culture of South Florida, the Invaders embody resilience, a bold reminder that some forces simply cannot be tamed. Welcome to the new era of jai alai – and brace for impact!

## Raging Bulls

Step aboard the thrilling voyage of the Raging Bulls, the embodiment of South Florida's vibrant essence and its other cherished pastime sport – sports fishing! Just like jai alai is no ordinary game, neither is the high-stakes hunt for the mightiest male Mahi – a prized catch that promises glory and bragging rights across the Atlantic's deep blues and jai alai courts across the land. The Raging Bulls carry the legacy of South Florida's golden era, echoing the allure of legendary boxing middleweight champ Jake “The Raging Bull" LaMotta. His life inside and outside of the ring, etched into the annals of history by Scorsese's masterpiece and immortalized by DeNiro's performance, now resonates with the tenacity of our sport. For those who yearn for the thrill of the catch and revere the iconic figures of a bygone era, the Raging Bulls are your clarion call to adventure.

Channeling the legendary spirits of Captain Kidd, Blackbeard, and the buccaneers who once claimed Florida's shores, our team is on a quest not just for the championship, but for the glory and riches that come with every win. Our team embodies that ruthless pirate drive, melding jai alai's Spanish heritage with Florida's rich history of maritime marauders. We're the villains of the court, infamous not for pillage and plunder, but for seizing every point, every match, and most of all, “to get as much bootie as they can in one night". Join us as we navigate through the season with the wind at our backs and our eyes on the prize. Here's to the renegades, the treasure seekers, the champions of the night. This team is for you — let's set the jai alai world ablaze and claim our treasure!
